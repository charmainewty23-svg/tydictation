📖 Game rules

2 minutes 45 seconds · 5 lives · you climb by reading the phrase out loud

1 · GOAL
Fly for 2 minutes 45 seconds. Read the phrase out loud — each word the app hears pulls the cat toward the gate opening.
Stop reading, read the wrong word, or read too slowly and the cat sinks.
2 · CONTROLS
🎤 Voice modeRead words = climb · stop = sink
👆 Practice modeHold screen / Space = fly
❤️ Lives5
Only voice mode scores on the all-time board. Practice mode has no ranking and no Paws — it is just for learning the flight.
3 · READ TO FLY
The phrase appears word by word. Say each word clearly; matched words turn green and pull the cat into the gate opening. Stop or misread and it sinks.
Volume alone does nothing — background noise will not lift you.
Each word heardpull toward the gate opening
Stop / wrong / too slowthe cat sinks

Orange wordsread again before the gate passes

4 · LEVELS — SHORT → DEEP
The level rises every 5 phrases you read. Deeper level = longer phrase, more points and a higher pass target. L1 is a short warm-up; L2 uses Discussion Phrases; L3 moves to Hot Topics; L4 mixes the longer sentences.
L1 · Warm-up (0 read)20 pts
L2 · Discussion (5 read)40 pts
L3 · Hot Topics (10 read)70 pts
L4 · Deep talk (15 read)100 pts
5 · BOUNCE-BACKS
Gate not read to 75%−1 life · bounce back · red words
Hit the gate frame−1 life · short shield
Touch spikes / water−1 life · float back up
First 6 secondsfree splash · no life lost
Run ends0 lives or 2 minutes 45 seconds
A missed gate never ends the run — the cat is only stunned. The words you missed stay red so you can read them again straight away. A splash floats the cat back up, so one bad moment cannot chain into three quick lives.
6 · SCORE & GHOST CAT
Score = points for each phrase you actually read (⭐ × 2 for the whole phrase). A phrase read with zero misses grows your 🔥 streak.
Your last best hovers in the sky as the 👻 Ghost Cat line. Cross it and the sky lights up 🎆.
Pace (WPM) is only counted when read accuracy is at least 80%.
7 · LEADERBOARD & PAWS
All-time boardTop 7 · personal best
Paws per phrase+3 🐾
Reaching the board+8 🐾 once
ResetNever · all-time best
The +8 board bonus only counts when you enter the all-time top 7 — staying there does not pay again. Drop off, then climb back in, and it counts once more.
8 · FAIR PLAY
Everyone gets the same 2 minutes 45 seconds, the same level ladder and the same phrases.
Points come from words the app hears you read — never from how loud the room is.
Practice (tap) runs are not ranked: no board entry, no Paws. Accuracy is coaching, not a punishment.

Purr-fect Survival

One class, one survival game. Choose 1–4 rounds.

🔒 Sign in with your school Google account to create or join a room. Guests can watch this page but cannot play.

Create a room

Round 1 · 1-2-3 Meow!

30–300 m, in steps of 10. Every correct answer still sprints +10 m and the time cap scales with the distance.

Round 2 · Glass Stepping Stones

3–15 steps. 7 seconds per step; a wrong or missed pane still drops you out.

Round 3 · Tug of War

30–120 seconds. Both teams answer at the same time; the golden salmon starts at the centre.

Round 4 · Dalgona Candy Carving

60–600 seconds (default 180). The tug winners carve a golden candy. Candy patterns assign the question types automatically: 🐟 Fishbone = New Vocab + Dictation Master spelling; 🐾 Cat paw = the five grammar topics; 👑 Crown cat = participle sentence combining (Q251+). One wrong carve is a crack, a second splits the candy.

Round 5 · Grammar Finals

60–180 seconds (default 120). Every Dalgona survivor enters the electric-fence arena. Error Spotting (6 s) and Double-Trap Cloze (8 s) pay the grammar bank. A wrong answer slips 25 back; a wrong answer at 0 is electrocuted. Every 3 correct answers fires a paw-wave that knocks the nearest opponent back 15. If fewer than 2 Dalgona survivors remain, the finals are skipped.

Each round can use a different subject and bank. The host sets the rounds, distance, glass steps and timers for the whole class.
